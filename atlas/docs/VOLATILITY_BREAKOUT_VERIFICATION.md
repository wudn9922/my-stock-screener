# Daily volatility and breakout entries — 2026-10-20

## Calculations and data

- ATR(20)% = Wilder ATR(20) / the same completed daily close × 100. True range is max(high-low, abs(high-previous close), abs(low-previous close)). Seed with the mean of the first 20 true ranges with known prior closes; update with (previous ATR × 19 + TR) / 20 across the available daily history.
- Mean intraday range = mean((high-low) / previous close × 100) across the most recent 20 completed daily bars. This excludes overnight gaps, unlike true range. Require at least 21 valid, increasing daily bars; missing history or a failed request displays an unavailable value.
- Daily data comes through the selected MarketDataProvider, regardless of the displayed chart interval. Session-close filtering uses the symbol's exchange calendar rules. The header never divides ATR by a potentially intraday quote. Data date, source, stale-cache status and price basis appear in the help disclosure. Missing sessions are not fabricated. Unadjusted corporate actions can inflate measured volatility.
- Swing-high entries use a confirmed local turn, such as the middle high of a 1-2-3 example, not an N-day maximum. The pivot is at least as high as its two left candles and strictly higher than its two right candles (last equal high on a plateau). It becomes available only after the right candles close, from the following bar. A strict intraday high > the latest confirmed pivot triggers entry even when the close falls back. No full 1-2-3, higher-low, volume or time-cooldown condition is added. A pivot persists until a more recent pivot is confirmed; fixed-age rolling maxima are not used.
- Each pivot contributes its first break. Different waves can contribute multiple events in one window, so signal counts can exceed stock counts. Swing entries use max(open, pivot) as a theoretical stop-fill approximation; neither the prior pivot on a gap-up nor a cheaper post-break close is an assumed fill. Daily bars cannot verify actual tick fills, minimum price increments, slippage or fees. Missing opens do not produce invented entries. Success uses the same report-date close and minimum three-trading-day maturity as other methods.
- The 20-session box retains existing width thresholds (TW 14%, US 10%). EMA20 requires prior close <= prior EMA and current close > current EMA, using an initial 20-close SMA and alpha 2/21. Box/EMA retain their ten-session cooldown and first-event-per-stock/window policy. Full-market reports expose exactly swingHigh/box20/ema20; old rolling-high results are never substituted for swing results.
- Entry success = profitable mature signals / mature signals, across the full-market exchange listings: TW listed/OTC ordinary shares and US ordinary shares/ADRs. Stocks without signals, insufficient histories and unmatured signals are not failures. The old unconditional market up-rate is no longer shown.
- `build_full_market_breakouts.py` reads the same lists.json, state.json and daily bars as the existing full-market screener. Listed-stock counts never shrink when CSV history is missing. Reports include listing date/source, loaded/missing histories, current valid bars and stale/non-trading stock counts; each strategy shows its own history-eligible count. List age must be at most 14 days; exchange lists must meet existing minimum sizes, and at least 90% of listed stocks must have valid report-date bars before either market is published. Incomplete input preserves the prior report and fails the job. No partial-list or S&P 500 fallback is used.
- `universe-full.yml` generates success rates after refreshing the full daily cache, and commits the report with the full screener universe. The earlier subset-only schedule step is removed. PR verification restores the existing full cache read-only, rebuilds the real report and uploads it as an artifact. A report without explicit full scope and consistent coverage is hidden by the UI.
